Why Keller Artificial Turf Works

Turf That Suits How You Use the Yard

Dogs, kids, a pool and a putting green each ask something different of turf. We talk through how your yard gets used and how much sun it gets, then match the base, product and infill to that, and we'll tell you plainly what turf is like on a July afternoon. You also get a 15-year warranty on materials and a 1-year warranty on labor.

Base Work Comes First

Every install starts with the ground. A compacted stone base is what keeps turf flat and draining on the clay soil common around Keller, so we spend our time there before any turf goes down.

Straight Talk About Heat

Turf gets warm in Texas sun. We look at shade and product color with you before anything goes in, and we'll tell you what to expect in July.

Pet Turf Built to Drain

Dog areas get a permeable backing over compacted stone, with infill options for odor, so rinsing down the run stays a quick job.
