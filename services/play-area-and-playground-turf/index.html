Common Questions

Play Area and Playground Turf: Common Questions

How much does playground turf cost in Keller?
Play area turf typically falls around $10 to $12 a square foot installed. The size of the area, the base prep and the turf grade decide the final figure, and the written quote has the number. A playground turf install also comes with a 15-year warranty on materials and a 1-year warranty on labor.
Does turf get too hot for kids in a Keller summer?
On a sunny afternoon, yes, it can be too warm for bare feet. Turf holds heat in direct sun where grass stays cooler, and North Texas has plenty of 100-degree days. Shade from a tree, sail or the house, a lighter-colored product and a hose rinse before playtime all help, and mornings and evenings are the comfortable times to play in high summer.
Is turf with a pad softer for falls?
A pad adds cushion compared with turf laid straight on the base. How much it helps depends on the pad's thickness and the height of the equipment above it, so check the playset maker's guidance for the surface it recommends. We can talk through pad options for your setup.
Can you put turf around an existing swing set?
Usually, yes. Many sets can stay where they are while we work around the posts, though larger ones sometimes need to be moved for base work and set back afterward. Send a photo of the set and how it's anchored so we can plan for it.
How do I clean playground turf?
A leaf blower or a stiff broom clears most debris, and a hose rinse takes care of dust, spilled drinks and sticky spots. Brushing the high-traffic area under the swings now and then helps the blades stand back up.
